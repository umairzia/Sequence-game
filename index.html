Sequence

How to play
  • Pick a card from your hand, then put a chip on a space showing that card. Every card is on the board twice.
  • The four corners are free: they count as a chip for everyone.
  • Jack of ♦ or ♣ (two eyes) is wild: put a chip on any open space.
  • Jack of ♠ or ♥ (one eye) removes one red chip, except chips in a finished sequence.
  • Five chips in a row (across, down or diagonal) is a sequence. Two sequences win. The second may share one chip with the first.
  • A card is dead when both of its spaces are taken. Once per turn, you can swap it for a new card before you play.
  • Turns go round the table: host, Bot A, guest, Bot B. Teammates can't see each other's cards.
